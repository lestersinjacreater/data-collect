Donate my SMSChangia SMS zangu

A one-time donation of your received messages to help the hackathon team train Linda to spot M-Pesa scams. Mchango wa mara moja wa jumbe ulizopokea ili kusaidia timu ya hackathon kufundisha Linda kutambua ulaghai wa M-Pesa.

Download for Android (APK) Pakua kwa Android (APK)

Android only. If the button does nothing, open the releases page. Android pekee. Kitufe kisipofanya kazi, fungua ukurasa wa matoleo.

How to installJinsi ya kusakinisha

  1. Tap the download button and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from this source (your browser). This is needed because the app is not on the Play Store.
  3. If Play Protect warns you, tap "More details" then "Install anyway".
  4. Open SMS Donate, read the notice, and follow the steps.
  1. Bonyeza kitufe cha kupakua na ufungue faili inapomaliza.
  2. Android ikiuliza, ruhusu usakinishaji kutoka chanzo hiki (kivinjari chako). Hii inahitajika kwa sababu programu haipo Play Store.
  3. Play Protect ikionya, bonyeza "Maelezo zaidi" kisha "Sakinisha hata hivyo".
  4. Fungua SMS Donate, soma notisi, na ufuate hatua.

What you are agreeing toUnachokubali

  • The app reads your received SMS once, on your phone.
  • Codes (OTP/PIN), ID numbers, card numbers and phone numbers are hidden before anything is saved.
  • You can leave out any sender, and you see examples before sharing.
  • The app has no internet access. It makes one spreadsheet file and you choose to send it to the team.
  • When you tap "Finish", the file is deleted from your phone.
  • It is voluntary. Do not install it if you would rather not.
  • Programu inasoma SMS ulizopokea mara moja, kwenye simu yako.
  • Misimbo (OTP/PIN), nambari za kitambulisho, kadi na simu hufichwa kabla ya kuhifadhi.
  • Unaweza kuacha nje mtumaji yeyote, na unaona mifano kabla ya kushiriki.
  • Programu haina intaneti. Inatengeneza faili moja na wewe unachagua kuituma kwa timu.
  • Ukibonyeza "Maliza", faili inafutwa kwenye simu yako.
  • Ni hiari. Usisakinishe ukipendelea hivyo.
